Privacy policy

This application is for the owner's personal use. It uses the Google Drive API to access file contents and metadata and to carry out file operations requested or configured by the owner.

rclone runs on the owner's computer and saves its OAuth tokens locally. Files are transferred between the owner's computer and the Google Drive account the owner authorizes. This website does not receive Drive files or OAuth tokens. The application does not sell Drive data, use it for advertising, or share it with other users.

The owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings and remove the local rclone configuration to delete stored tokens.
